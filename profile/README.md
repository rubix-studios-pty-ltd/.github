# Rubix Studios

Rubix Studios is an Australian digital agency established in 2001, specializing in branding, marketing, web development, and multimedia production. With offices in Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane, we deliver integrated digital solutions for businesses across various industries.

## Services

- **Branding**: Development of distinctive brand identities, including logos, visual assets, and brand guidelines.
- **Marketing**: Execution of data-driven strategies encompassing SEO, Google Ads, social media campaigns, and content marketing.
- **Web Development**: Design and development of responsive websites, custom applications, and e-commerce platforms with full-stack capabilities.
- **Media Production**: Professional photography and videography services tailored for corporate, commercial, and promotional purposes.
- **Platform Solutions**: Creation of scalable applications and system integrations to enhance business operations and user engagement.
