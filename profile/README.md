# Rubix Studios

**Branding, Marketing & Web Development**

Rubix Studios is an Australian digital agency established in 2001, specializing in branding, marketing, web development, and multimedia production. With offices in Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane, we deliver integrated digital solutions for businesses across various industries.

## Services

- **Branding**  
  Development of distinctive brand identities, including logos, visual assets, and brand guidelines.

- **Marketing**  
  Execution of data-driven strategies encompassing SEO, Google Ads, social media campaigns, and content marketing.

- **Web Development**  
  Design and development of responsive websites, custom applications, and e-commerce platforms with full-stack capabilities.

- **Media Production**  
  Professional photography and videography services tailored for corporate, commercial, and promotional purposes.

- **Platform Solutions**  
  Creation of scalable applications and system integrations to enhance business operations and user engagement.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React, Next.js, Vue.js, TypeScript  
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js, Prisma  
- **CMS**: WordPress, Strapi, Sanity, Storyblok, Payload  
- **E-commerce**: Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Medusa  
- **Databases**: MongoDB, MySQL, Supabase, Firebase  
- **APIs**: GraphQL, RESTful APIs  
- **DevOps**: Docker, CI/CD pipelines, cPanel  
- **Cloud Services**: AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure  
- **Other Tools**: Kong, Drizzle, Expo

## Company Overview

Since its inception, Rubix Studios has evolved into a full-service digital agency, offering a complete suite of services from strategy and design to development and deployment. Our in-house team ensures consistency and quality across all projects, catering to clients ranging from startups to international enterprises.
